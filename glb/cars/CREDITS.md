# Car models

Low-poly car bundle by **Quaternius** — released into the public domain under
**CC0 1.0**, no attribution required (credited here anyway).

- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/cars.html — mirrored at
  https://poly.pizza/bundle/Cars-Bundle-FE5IWe6OMk
- `coupe.glb` — "Sports Car 2"
- `sedan.glb` — "Normal Car 1"
- `suv.glb`   — "SUV"

The files are unmodified as downloaded. Everything the game layers on them —
paint colour, tinted glass, emissive optics, wheels re-pivoted so they roll —
happens at load time in `cars.js`.
