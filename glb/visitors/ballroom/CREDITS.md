# Ballroom guests

Distinct Mixamo characters used only for the cruise ballroom crowd (not the quartet).

- Source: Adobe Mixamo character library, mirrored as GLB in the [Mixamo Animations and Characters](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tanish434/Mixamo-Animations-Characters) dataset (`character_refined_glb/`).
- Characters: Joe, Louise, Martha, Leonard, Lewis, Elizabeth, Sophie, Brian.
- Idle/walk clips: Mixamo skeleton clips bundled in three.js `Xbot.glb` (Adobe Mixamo).
- Licence: Mixamo characters and animations are royalty-free for personal and commercial projects (games, film, illustration). Do not redistribute the raw character files as a standalone stock pack.
- Local copies are texture-resized (1024, JPEG/PNG) so each GLB stays web-sized.
